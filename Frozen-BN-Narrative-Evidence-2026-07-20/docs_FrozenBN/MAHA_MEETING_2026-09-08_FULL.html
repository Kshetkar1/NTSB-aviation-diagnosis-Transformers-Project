How it runs, end to end

Nine steps. Steps 1–3 happen once. Steps 4–9 run per accident.

1  Build1,742 accidents, 1982–2006. Coded fields only.
2  FreezeGraph and all probability tables fixed. Never retrained.
3  IndexThe 1,288 narratives from those same years, for search only.
4  New accidentA 2007–2019 narrative. Never seen before.
5  RedactStrip sentences stating the outcome. Keep mechanism words.
6  Read two waysPhrase-match the NTSB vocabulary. Find the 100 most similar past accidents.
7  Build evidenceNeighbours' coded events and outcomes become evidence on named nodes.
8  One propagationAll evidence loaded, inference runs once. Tables do not change.
9  GradeTop-1 injury, damage, cause vs the NTSB's codes.
The text never enters the network. Only named evidence nodes do.

What did not work

  • The network adds no severity accuracy. It matches plain neighbour voting on all 296 — identical to three decimals, confidence intervals included.
  • Events through the network give 57.7% on cause vs retrieval's 84.2%.
  • Two baselines beat us. TF-IDF injury Macro-F1 0.603 vs our 0.470. Supervised embedding LR on cause, 88.1% vs our 84.2%.
  • Fire cross-inference failed below chance. ROC AUC 0.39–0.46; retrieval on the same text reaches 0.97.
  • Merging event and severity evidence collapses to 38.5% / 41.9% — the same narrative counted twice.
  • Asking the LLM for probabilities directly is unusable. Calibration error 0.74 vs 0.12; cause ranking inverts.
4
Say — present as design, not apology
"Why does the BN add nothing?" Severity nodes are leaves; the neighbour distribution is applied as a likelihood ratio directly at those nodes; event evidence reaches them only by weaker paths. The network mediates the retrieval signal losslessly — it neither adds nor destroys it. That's a d-separation consequence, not a disappointing result.
"Why did fire fail?" Structural: fire has 13 ancestors out of 783 nodes, 6 barred by the leak guard, and the only one the parser ever reaches is the generic flightcrew node. It was never d-connected to the evidence.

Two different failures, don't merge them. Fatal has real separation (3.9×) but n=3 — unevaluable, and I'd say so rather than report 0%. Minor injury has 16 test cases and essentially no separation: base rate 10.3%, and even when minor is the truth retrieval only reaches 0.122.

If he asks "can you fix it": reweighting by base rate recovers fatal but not minor, and the Macro-F1 gain rests on a single accident — too thin to put in the paper, and it would need a validation split carved from 1982–2006, not the test set. Minor injury needs a different signal entirely.
