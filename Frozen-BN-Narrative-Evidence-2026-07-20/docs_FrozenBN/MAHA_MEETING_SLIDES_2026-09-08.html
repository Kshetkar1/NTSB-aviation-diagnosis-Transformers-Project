backup
Only if he asks "where does it break?"
Lead with the blunt version: on injury it is effectively a two-class predictor. Across all 296 accidents it outputs only "none" or "serious." P(minor) never exceeds 0.211 anywhere in the test set and P(fatal) never exceeds 0.168, against 0.91 and 0.98 for the two that win — neither can ever take an argmax.
Two different failures, keep them apart. Fatal has real separation but n=3, so it's unevaluable in this window. Minor injury has 16 cases and essentially no separation.
If he asks whether you can fix it: reweighting by base rate recovers fatal but not minor, and the Macro-F1 gain rests on a single accident — too thin to publish, and picking the weight on the test set is the thing you've avoided everywhere else. Minor injury needs a different signal.
Also true and worth having ready: the BN doesn't add accuracy over plain neighbour voting on severity, because the evidence is constructed as L(j) = f(j)/p₀(j), which cancels the network's own prior exactly — there's a self-test asserting it to 1e-6. The network mediates the retrieval signal without distorting it. That's a design choice, not a disappointment.
